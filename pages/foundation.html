Giving the work a structure

The Daniel Mutai Foundation.

For most of the last 18 years, Daniel's humanitarian work has run informally — funded personally, coordinated through well-wishers, and carried out alongside a full-time teaching role. The Daniel Mutai Foundation exists to give that work a lasting structure: a formal vehicle through which partners, donors and institutions can engage with the mobility, education, health and community programmes documented across this portfolio.

Formalising the work matters for three reasons: it lets partners fund a specific programme rather than an individual, it creates accountability through documented projects and reports, and it gives the work continuity beyond any one person.

What the Foundation covers

The same five pillars, under one structure.

Mobility & assistive devices

Coordinating the sourcing and delivery of wheelchairs, canes and other mobility aids.

Inclusive education

Supporting children with disabilities to access and stay in school.

School water & sanitation

Water and sanitation infrastructure projects in partner schools.

Eye health

Referral and support for people needing eye care.
