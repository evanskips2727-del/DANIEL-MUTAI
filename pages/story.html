The person behind the work

A teacher whose service grew beyond the classroom.

Teacher · Kenya

Daniel Mutai's public account begins with a moment of personal hardship: while sitting his KCSE examinations in 1999, he collapsed and was diagnosed with diabetes. Rather than defining the years that followed, that diagnosis became the backdrop to a life built around teaching and service to others.

He began teaching as an untrained teacher, later pursuing formal training in Early Childhood Development Education. During his teaching practice, he bought a wheelchair for a pupil in need — an experience he identifies as the pivotal moment that shaped the rest of his humanitarian work.

The journey

A timeline of service.

1988–1995

Primary education

Kenya Certificate of Primary Education (KCPE) — Chebanyiny Primary School.

1996–1999

Secondary education, and a turning point

Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) — Chebunyo Secondary School. According to his public account, Daniel collapsed while sitting his KCSE examinations and was diagnosed with diabetes.

2007–2009

Teacher training begins

Certificate in Primary Teacher Education (PTE) — Bomet Teachers Training College.

2009

Census work

Clerk, Kenya National Bureau of Statistics — national census.

2009–2010

Specialising in early childhood education

Diploma in Early Childhood Development and Education (ECDE) — Bomet Teachers Training College.

2010–2013

Teaching at Chepkorgong

BOM Teacher, Chepkorgong Primary School. Around this period, Daniel identifies facilitating a wheelchair for a pupil in need as the pivotal moment that shaped his humanitarian work.

2010–2019

National examinations service

Invigilator for the Kenya National Examinations Council (KCPE & KCSE), alongside his teaching duties.

2011–2018

Training the next generation of teachers

ECDE Part-Time Tutor, Bomet Teachers Training College.

2013–2016

School leadership at Kamorir Primary

Acting BOM Head Teacher (2013–2014), then Acting Deputy Head Teacher (2014–2016), Kamorir Primary School.

2014–present

Assistant Teacher, Kamorir Primary School

Daniel's current substantive teaching post, held alongside his humanitarian work.

Ongoing
