The person behind the work

A teacher whose service grew beyond the classroom.

Teacher · Kenya

Daniel Mutai's public account begins with a moment of personal hardship: while sitting his KCSE examinations in 1999, he collapsed and was diagnosed with diabetes. Rather than defining the years that followed, that diagnosis became the backdrop to a life built around teaching and service to others.

He began teaching as an untrained teacher, later pursuing formal training in Early Childhood Development Education. During his teaching practice, he bought a wheelchair for a pupil in need — an experience he identifies as the pivotal moment that shaped the rest of his humanitarian work.
