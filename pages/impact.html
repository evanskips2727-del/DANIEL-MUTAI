Method

How a number earns its place here.

This portfolio treats every statistic as a claim that should be checkable. The process behind each figure: capture the activity as it happens, attach the strongest available evidence, publish it without exposing sensitive beneficiary details, and track outcomes over time rather than only activities. The full system is set out in the Evidence Library.
