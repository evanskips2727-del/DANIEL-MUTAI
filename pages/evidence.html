As the record grows, every major claim on this site will be linked to source material here: certificates, project reports, photographs, partner confirmations or independent media coverage.

How it works

Document, verify, publish, measure.

1. Document

Capture the project, date, location and intervention as it happens.

2. Verify

Attach appropriate supporting evidence and consent from those involved.

3. Publish

Tell the story without exposing sensitive beneficiary information.

4. Measure

Track outcomes over time rather than only activities.
